# Sources and evidence limits

## Attribution

The decision cases distill teaching from the sixteen public educational videos below. Creator examples and arguments are not independent studies. The main workflow, counterexamples, review probes and cross-source comparisons are synthesis: they should be tested against the actual brief and product rather than attributed to a creator as exact quotations.

All sixteen source texts were read. Three videos had fixed visual samples at twenty-second intervals; eleven more had useful targeted interface scenes; the premium-app and typography videos had limited graphics/presenter evidence rather than chapter-level visual verification. This is not continuous viewing or a complete interaction audit. Transcript output did not establish caption authorship or native timed segments; no precise quotation timing is claimed.

Screenshots can support placement, grouping and visible content. They cannot prove persistence, focus return, easing, frame rate, error recovery, service promises or user performance. Ads and sponsor scenes are not interface evidence. No growth, conversion, ranking or research percentage in a creator's narration is adopted as a verified result. Publication dates and external research cited by creators were not independently checked.

## Conflicts to keep visible

- Minimal surfaces versus more information: optimize the task, not the count of visible elements.
- Strict spacing scales versus optical judgment: consistency helps, but a number does not override content, script or accessibility.
- Expressive motion versus quiet feedback: preserve the brief's creative purpose while keeping states and actions legible.
- Progressive disclosure versus discovery: hide secondary detail only when people can find it with their actual input method.
- Precise reassurance versus honesty: show a precise consequence only when supported; otherwise expose uncertainty.
- Formulaic theme conversion versus legibility: verify the rendered colors and states rather than assuming a lightness transform passes.

Further sources can extend these cases when actually inspected. Do not invent missing examples or turn a collection size into a claim of complete design knowledge.
